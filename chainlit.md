# Study Helper

A multi-agent exam-prep tutor. Ask a math or science question and it routes you to
the right specialist, or type `quiz` to start a timed quiz.

- **Math** — worked step by step, every number checked with a calculator tool.
- **Science** — physics, chemistry, biology and earth science, with a web search
  for facts that may have changed.
- **Quiz** — one question at a time under a time budget you set, ending with your
  score and the topics worth revising.

Ask away.
